Reading Adaptive Path's article on [Marking Strategy Tanglible](http://www.adaptivepath.com/ideas/newsletter/archives/063009/index.php), I came across a brilliant definition of strategy. While the dictionary defines strategy as:

> a plan of action or policy designed to achieve a major or overall aim

Jared Cole provides a more practical definition:

> … a way of communicating the value of a pursuing a particular goal, the activities required to attain the goal, and the ways that leadership will guide the process.
